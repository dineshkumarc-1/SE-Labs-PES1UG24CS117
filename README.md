## Lab 1 Summary: Community Solar Credit Allocation Manager

### Overview
The **Community Solar Credit Allocation Manager** provides a distributed accounting and allocation platform for residential microgrids, allowing prosumers to automatically ingest smart meter telemetry, manage solar credits, transfer surplus energy units to eligible community members, and integrate directly with utility billing statements.
